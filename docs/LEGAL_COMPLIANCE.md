# Flowtick — Legal, Privacy, Accessibility & Trust Checklist

> This is a production-readiness checklist, not a substitute for qualified legal advice.

## Core legal pages
Before public launch, review whether the site needs:
- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Use
- Cookie Policy
- Refund / Cancellation Policy
- Contact / Business Details
- Accessibility Statement

## Privacy
- Collect only necessary data.
- Explain what is collected and why.
- Document third parties.
- Document retention.
- Provide contact details for privacy questions.
- Do not invent legal commitments.

## Flowtick V1
If V1 stores todos only in browser localStorage and uses no analytics, remote APIs, forms, payments, or third-party tracking, avoid collecting personal data.

Do not add a fake cookie banner just because other websites have one.

## Forms
For every form:
- ask only for necessary fields
- explain why data is requested
- link to the Privacy Policy where appropriate
- do not pre-check optional marketing consent
- keep optional marketing consent separate

## Cookies and tracking
Audit:
- cookies
- localStorage
- sessionStorage
- analytics SDKs
- advertising pixels
- session replay
- chat widgets
- embedded video
- social embeds
- maps
- CAPTCHA
- third-party fonts/scripts

## Consumer protection
Remove:
- fake reviews
- fake testimonials
- fake user counts
- fake awards
- fake press logos
- fake scarcity timers
- unsupported claims
- unsupported security guarantees

## Copyright and licences
Audit:
- images
- illustrations
- icons
- fonts
- videos
- audio
- 3D models
- code snippets/components
- templates
- logos/trademarks
- AI-generated assets

## Accessibility baseline
Target WCAG 2.2 AA.

Check:
- alt text
- color contrast
- keyboard operation
- visible focus
- labels
- form errors
- reduced motion

## Nigeria
Review applicable requirements under:
- Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023
- current NDPC guidance
- Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act where consumers are involved

Flag uncertainty instead of silently assuming compliance.
